APDCL BoM Kits

Standard items and quantities for one unit of work — a stay, a pole, a route-km, a substation. Set the number of units and the work type to get quantities and a cost abstract in the office A–L format.

Rates are ex-works SoR 2021-22, excl. GST. CHECK marks a rate where your notes and the office estimate sheets disagree — confirm against the SoR booklet. E marks a labour-included job item (grouting, fencing, plinth, boring) that belongs in row E, not row A.
Quantity sources: REC construction standards via APDCL DDUGJY/VEI tender BoQ, the 2026 Jamuguri 33 kV NIT, and Samaguri ESD office estimates. Departmental work: F = G = 0. Deposit work: F = 3% of C, G = 15% of (D+E). Railway way-leave and non-SoR items are not included.
