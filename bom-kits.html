APDCL BoM Kits

Standard items and quantities for one unit of work — a stay, a pole, a route-km, a bay. Set the number of units and the work type; every rate is read from the SoR 2021-22 sheet your estimates use.

Rates: SoR 2021-22 ex-works, excl. GST, read from the same SoR sheet as Line Survey (MVCC as per Corrigendum No-2; DTR fencing as per CGM(PP&D) memo 1150 dt 06.03.2024). E marks a labour-included job item that goes in row E, not row A. NOT IN SoR marks an approved rate outside the SoR.
Polymeric pin insulators (626 / 613) come with the GI pin fitted, so no 841 / 840 pins are listed. Departmental, O&M and calamity work: F = G = 0. Deposit work, utility shifting and vehicular damage: F = 3% of C, G = 15% of D + 2.25% of E. Railway way-leave and other non-SoR charges are not included.
