The Quality Assurance Manual (QAM) is what NQM/RQM inspectors use to grade your work. Learn the three defect categories, drill on real defects, and use the checklists on site before you ask for an inspection.

Ratings and sizes from the RDSS technical specification, the REC amendments and the standard drawings. These are the numbers that decide which item goes into the estimate.

Built from: RDSS SBD Part-2 Technical Specification (2022), the RDSS/REC standard tender drawings (11KV, 33KV, DTR-SS, PSS, LT-ACC-ABC, GEN, LT-OH, Civil), REC construction-standard amendments (2004), the new specifications (Attachments I–M) and the DDUGJY Quality Assurance Manual. Rates are APDCL SoR 2021-22 ex-works, excluding GST; items shown “—” have no SoR rate here and need the approved or tender rate. In “RDSS / REC drawing” mode the kits follow the standard drawings; “APDCL SoR” mode swaps in the items APDCL estimates use (9.75 m PSC, T-arm, grouting jobs, CI pipes). The site, the circle's approved drawings and the GTPs decide the final count.
