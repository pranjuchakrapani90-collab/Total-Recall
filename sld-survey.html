Rules for this survey

Intermediate poles are added so that no span exceeds these lengths.

Structures: deviation under 10° is a tangent pole; 10° or more is a cut-point with one stay, over 60° two stays. Dead-ends get one stay, tee-offs one per branch. Every new pole gets a grouting job and every stay a stay grouting job.
